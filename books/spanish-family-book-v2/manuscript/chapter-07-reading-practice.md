## Story 1 · Maya's Morning / La mañana de Maya

Maya wakes up on Monday. She is happy because Monday is library day. In the kitchen, her mother makes eggs and bread. Maya drinks milk and eats one banana. "Thank you, Mom!" she says. She puts her notebook and two pencils in her blue backpack. At the door, Sol the dog wags his tail. "Goodbye, Sol! See you later!" Maya runs to school. The clock in her classroom says eight o'clock. She is early and excited.

*Maya despierta el lunes. Está feliz porque el lunes es el día de la biblioteca. En la cocina, su mamá hace huevos y pan. Maya bebe leche y come un plátano. "¡Gracias, mamá!", dice. Pone su cuaderno y dos lápices en su mochila azul. En la puerta, Sol, el perro, mueve la cola. "¡Adiós, Sol! ¡Hasta luego!" Maya corre a la escuela. El reloj de su salón marca las ocho. Llega temprano y emocionada.*

New words in this story / Palabras nuevas en este cuento: wakes up = despierta · library = biblioteca · early = temprano · wags his tail = mueve la cola

## Story 2 · Leo's School Day / El día de escuela de Leo

Leo loves Tuesday because Tuesday is art day. He brings his crayons, his glue and one big piece of paper. His teacher says, "Good morning, class! Today we paint our families." Leo paints four people: his mother, his father, his sister Maya and his grandfather. He paints Sol too, because Sol is family. The colors are red, yellow, green and brown. "Who is this?" asks the teacher, pointing at the brown dog. "That is Sol!" says Leo. "He is the best dog in the world." The class laughs and claps.

*A Leo le encanta el martes porque el martes es el día de arte. Trae sus crayones, su pegamento y un papel grande. Su maestra dice: "¡Buenos días, clase! Hoy pintamos a nuestras familias." Leo pinta a cuatro personas: su mamá, su papá, su hermana Maya y su abuelo. También pinta a Sol, porque Sol es familia. Los colores son rojo, amarillo, verde y café. "¿Quién es este?", pregunta la maestra, señalando al perro café. "¡Ese es Sol!", dice Leo. "Es el mejor perro del mundo." La clase ríe y aplaude.*

New words in this story / Palabras nuevas en este cuento: piece of paper = hoja de papel · paints = pinta · pointing at = señalando · claps = aplaude

## Story 3 · Saturday at the Park / Sábado en el parque

On Saturday, the whole family goes to the park. Father brings water and juice. Mother brings bread, cheese and apples. Maya and Nina play tag near the big tree, and Sol runs faster than everybody. Leo sits under the tree with his grandfather and reads a book about dogs. "Why do you love dogs?" asks the grandfather. "Because they are happy and friendly," says Leo, "like our family." At five o'clock everybody is tired and hungry. They eat together on the green grass. "This is the best Saturday," says Maya. "Yes," says Sol with his eyes.

*El sábado, toda la familia va al parque. Papá trae agua y jugo. Mamá trae pan, queso y manzanas. Maya y Nina juegan atrapadas cerca del árbol grande, y Sol corre más rápido que todos. Leo se sienta bajo el árbol con su abuelo y lee un libro sobre perros. "¿Por qué te encantan los perros?", pregunta el abuelo. "Porque son felices y amigables", dice Leo, "como nuestra familia." A las cinco, todos están cansados y con hambre. Comen juntos en el pasto verde. "Este es el mejor sábado", dice Maya. "Sí", dice Sol con sus ojos.*

New words in this story / Palabras nuevas en este cuento: everybody = todos · grass = pasto · faster than = más rápido que · near = cerca de

### Activity 7.4 · Family Practice: Read-Aloud Night / Práctica familiar: Noche de lectura en voz alta
