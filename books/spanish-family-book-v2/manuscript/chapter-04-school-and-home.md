| English | Say it | Español |

### Activity 4.3 · In, On, or Under / In, on o under

Fill in each blank with in, on, or under.

*Completa cada espacio con in, on o under.*

1. The pencils are ______ my backpack. (inside it / *adentro*)
2. The lamp is ______ the table. (touching the top / *encima, tocando*)
3. The dog sleeps ______ the bed. (below it / *debajo*)
4. My clothes are ______ the drawer. (inside it / *adentro*)
5. The clock is ______ the wall, above the board. (touching the surface / *sobre la superficie*)

### Activity 4.6 · Family Practice: Home Scavenger Hunt / Práctica familiar: Búsqueda del tesoro en casa
