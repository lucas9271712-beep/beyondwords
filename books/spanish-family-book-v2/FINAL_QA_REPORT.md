## 7. Remaining risks

- **Human review required:** editorial, educational (age-progress check by a teacher/parent), and visual proof inspection. Model reviews on file are explicitly non-independent.
- **Cover template:** geometry follows KDP formulas exactly, but the official cover-calculator template (account-gated) was not overlaid; verify hinge shift and barcode placement in KDP Previewer.
- **RGB color space:** files are RGB; KDP converts to CMYK and removes profiles - slight color variance possible on press.
- **EPUB:** structurally self-checked but not EPUBCheck-validated; workbook tables may reflow imperfectly on e-ink. Print is the primary product.
- **Keywords/categories:** content-fit proposals with unverified search support (no live capture tooling here).
- **AI disclosure:** both text and images must be disclosed as AI-generated at KDP title setup.
- **Project-store divergence:** the revision-3 store from the owner's Windows workspace is not present in this sandbox; this environment's store was initialized fresh (see 8).
